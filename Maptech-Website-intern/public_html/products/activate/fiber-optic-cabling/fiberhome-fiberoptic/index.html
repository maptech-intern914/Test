FiberHome Unitube

Designed for high fiber density and reliable access-network installation, this cable combines a small outer diameter with environmental and mechanical protection.

Features

  • Maximum core count: 24
  • Small outer diameter and high fiber density
  • UV resistant and water blocking
  • Superior mechanical and environmental performance
  • Flame retardant, low smoke, and non-toxic
  • Dielectric construction with anti-electromagnetic interference

Applications

  • Duct installation
  • Indoor access

Installation Performance

  • Minimum bend radius: 20D during installation; 10D during operation
  • Temperature range: storage -40 to +70 °C; installation -10 to +50 °C; operation -20 to +70 °C
